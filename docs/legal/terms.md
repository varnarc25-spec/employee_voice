## The service

Employee Voice India is an independent discussion space for IT employees in India. You can read and post in topic categories, vote in polls, and, if you choose, record interest in helping explore a future employee organization.

The service is **not**:

- a government programme or an official labour portal
- a registered trade union
- a statutory membership register
- a law firm or a source of legal advice
- an emergency service

Recording organizing interest does not make you a member of a trade union and does not appoint anyone to represent you.

## Who may join

You must confirm that you are 18 or older and that you agree to these terms and the privacy notice. We do not independently verify your age. You are responsible for the accuracy of that confirmation.

Employment verification is optional and separate from sign-in. Failing to complete it does not, by itself, close your account.

## Your account

Sign-in uses an email link from the configured email provider. Do not forward sign-in links. You can revoke sessions, deactivate, or delete the account as described in the privacy notice. You must not create an account for someone else without their permission, or try to bypass rate limits or access controls.

## Content

You keep responsibility for what you post. You grant the operator a non-exclusive licence to host, display, and moderate that content for running the community. Do not post another person's contact details, government identity numbers, private messages, or information that identifies a co-worker in a way that can harm them. The community guidelines are part of these terms.

We may remove content that breaks the guidelines. You can appeal a removal. Moderation is not a court and not a union grievance procedure.

## Polls

You may cast one vote in each poll. Aggregated results are shown only after the poll closes and only when at least five ballots exist. We do not promise that your choice is cryptographically anonymous.

## Organizing interest

The Get Involved flow is optional. It is not required to use the rest of the community. Withdrawal stops the active interest record and does not delete your ordinary account.

## Disclaimers

Workplace posts are personal accounts from members, not findings by the operator. The operator does not endorse employers, unions, political parties, or government bodies. To the extent counsel confirms it is lawful, the operator is not liable for member content or for decisions you make based on it. A counsel-reviewed limitation of liability and governing-law clause must replace this paragraph before launch. This draft intentionally does not pick a forum or a statute of limitations.

## Changes

We may update these terms by publishing a new version. Where a change affects a consent we record, we will ask for a new acknowledgement before recording it.
