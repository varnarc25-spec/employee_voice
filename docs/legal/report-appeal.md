# Reports and appeals (draft)

**Status: draft for review by qualified Indian counsel before publication. Timeframes below are operational targets, not statutory deadlines.**

**Version:** draft-2026-10-16

## How to report

In the app, open a post or comment and choose Report. Pick a reason:

- harassment
- spam
- personal data
- misleading
- safety
- other

Add a short explanation without repeating phone numbers, emails, or identity numbers. You can also report a member from their content.

Blocking hides that member's posts and comments from you. It does not remove the content for everyone and it does not tell the other person.

## What moderators see

A moderator sees the report reason, your explanation, and the reported content, because they cannot decide without it. They do not see organizing-interest records. Opening a report is audited.

## Decisions

A moderator can:

- **dismiss** the report and leave the content up
- **remove** the content from the community
- **escalate** the report to administrators

Administrators decide escalated reports. Upholding a report about a person does not automatically delete that account. Account deactivation is a separate administrator action and is audited.

We aim to review new reports within a few days. That is a staffing target, not a legal promise.

## Appeals

If your content was removed, you can submit one appeal from the app. Explain why it should be restored. Do not include new personal data.

A pending appeal does not put the content back. If the appeal is accepted, the content is published again. If it is denied, it stays removed. Escalated appeals are decided by an administrator.

## What this process is not

This is not a labour-court filing, a police complaint, or an internal company grievance. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. The app cannot do that for you.

## Your own copy

You can request an export of your account data in the app. The export includes reports you filed. It does not include how you voted, because that choice is not stored with your account id.
