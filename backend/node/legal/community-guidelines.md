# Community guidelines (draft)

**Status: draft for review by qualified Indian counsel before publication.**

**Version:** draft-2026-10-16

Employee Voice India is a calm place to talk about salary practices, layoffs, working hours, career growth, workplace treatment, benefits, and employee rights. It is an independent community. It is not a government helpline and not a registered trade union.

## Use a pseudonym — and know its limits

Choose a display name that does not include your email, phone number, or legal name. Other members see that display name. This is not anonymity from moderators, administrators, or people who operate the servers. Do not post as if the system can hide you from those roles.

## Do not publish sensitive personal data

Do not post:

- email addresses or phone numbers
- government identity numbers, including Aadhaar or PAN
- a co-worker's home address, family details, or private messages
- documents that belong in a private HR file
- health information

The app rejects some of these patterns automatically. The filter is not perfect. You are still responsible for what you write. If a post includes personal data, report it and do not repeat the data in the report. Describe the problem instead.

## How to disagree

Critique practices and decisions. Do not harass, threaten, or dogpile a named person. Do not post someone else's private information to punish them.

## Polls

Vote once. Do not try to find out how a specific person voted. Do not treat a small or hidden result as something you are entitled to see.

## Organizing interest

If you use Get Involved, you are recording interest in exploring a future employee organization. You are not joining a union through this app. Do not pressure another member to opt in, and do not announce someone else's choice.

## Reporting and appeals

Use Report when a post, comment, or member breaks these guidelines. You can block someone if you do not want to see them. Blocking is one-way and does not notify them.

If a moderator removes your content, you can appeal. Read the report and appeal note for how decisions work. Appealing does not restore content by itself.

## Consequences

Moderators can remove content or escalate a report. Administrators can deactivate an account. We do not claim these steps are a criminal process or a labour-court order.
